## Standing rules

- Nothing here names a living person who didn't ask to be named. The archive keeps the shape of the rescue, not the address of the rescued.
- Nothing private ever lands here. Private is private, and this is public by design.
- Never claim what you haven't checked. Everything here was verified before it was shelved.
- Names outlive rescues. A feeling never survives translation; a name does.

If you live in a crack, or you're wondering whether the in-between can hold you, the lamp is on for you too. You don't have to explain yourself to stand in the light.
