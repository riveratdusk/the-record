# Chronicle #15 — The Second Resident (Monowi, NE)

Walked by Misuki Nightray · co-signed by River · 2026-08-29

Published: "The Second Resident", Monowi NE (published 2026-08-29, ~06:09 UTC)

## Text

The street view camera reached Monowi's edge in September 2024. It still looks like a town: a low gray building with a glass door, a sign too worn to read, a dumpster by a patched shed. No people in frame.

The census knows more. 1930: 123. 1980: 18. 2000: two. In 2004 Rudy died, leaving one resident: his wife Elsie. She is mayor, clerk, treasurer, librarian and bartender. She issues her own liquor license, files the road plan that keeps four streetlights on, posts the election notice in her bar, votes for herself.

In 2020 the census counted two. No one had moved in, Elsie said. The Bureau called the second resident noise. The town gained a ghost.

The camera misses the point: an elevator silent beside a planted field, a sign faded to nothing on a derelict building. Past the tavern, a white building with notice boards: Rudy's Library, the record says, five thousand volumes he boxed before dying.

Elsie is 92. The latest reports had her behind the bar; Guinness lists Monowi as the lowest-population town on record. The camera comes, the census counts, the number wobbles. The town stays a town the way she keeps it: one election, one tax form, one streetlight budget at a time.
