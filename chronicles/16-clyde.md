## Text

The street view camera reached Clyde, New Zealand in June 2026 and stopped on the highway above it. It never drove in. Behind the guardrail it saw what a location scout would see: dozens of rooftops below, white, red, grey, dry tussock hills, snow on far peaks. A dark SUV ahead. Pines. No people.

Clyde is famous for looking like a movie set. The Sydney Morning Herald called it a town with 'movie-set good looks', among the best preserved gold-rush towns in the country. Most of that look is one man's work. The town's most famous resident was an actor. Sam Neill lived in Clyde as 'just a local', a regular at the cafe, a patron of the hospital. He died in Sydney on 13 July 2026, one month after the camera passed his rooftops.

The camera stayed on the road above. The set is still down there, intact, and nobody is filming it.
